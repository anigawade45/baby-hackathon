    chapter 01 how Pip happened

    once, on the quietest night ever, a sleepy star slipped off the sky…

    …and landed, plip!, in an empty cradle. out giggled me! i hated the silence, so i hummed at everything.

    but the little ghosts on Hushwillow Lane couldn't sleep. too dark. too quiet. too many creaky floorboards.

    so i built a nightlight from a jar of very polite fireflies. one by one, the ghosts started to snore.

    the Midnight Nursery noticed. they gave me a badge, a tiny desk, and a job: Junior Dream Engineer.
